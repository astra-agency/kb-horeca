# Case Study: Interactive Personalised Sales Proposals

## Context

Most hotel sales teams send static PDF attachments or plain-text emails with a rate sheet attached. This kind of "proposal" ignores the specific dates, guest composition, and current pricing — the guest receives a generic document and goes to compare prices on an OTA instead.

## Objective

Automate proposal generation so that every document is built from the specific deal data: check-in/out dates, room category, guest composition, applicable promotions, and live pricing — including a discount, a countdown timer, and a direct booking link.

## What Was Done

### 1. Interactive Web-Based Format

The proposal is generated not as a PDF but as a personalised web page delivered via a unique link. The guest sees:
- room category with photos and description,
- check-in and check-out dates,
- guest composition (adults / children),
- pricing with the standard rate crossed out and the discount applied,
- included services (breakfast, transfers, activities, spa access),
- a countdown timer showing how long the offer is valid,
- a "Book Now" button linking directly to the payment page.

The link works on any device — no PDF viewer, no attachment, no friction.

### 2. Auto-Generation from the CRM

When a deal is moved to the correct pipeline stage, the proposal is generated automatically. The system pulls all data from the deal card — the sales manager does not assemble it manually.

### 3. Engagement Tracking

The system logs:
- whether the guest opened the link,
- time spent on the page,
- whether they clicked through to payment.

The sales manager receives a notification when the guest opens the proposal — enabling a perfectly timed follow-up call rather than cold outreach.

## Results

- Proposal-to-booking conversion improves: guests receive a personalised offer rather than a rate card.
- Sales managers spend a fraction of the previous time preparing proposals.
- The countdown timer creates urgency and accelerates decision-making.
- Manual data entry errors (wrong dates, wrong pricing) are eliminated.

## Key Considerations

- Proposal templates need to be developed for each room category and guest type (families, couples, corporate, groups).
- Live pricing integration from the PMS or booking engine is essential — otherwise someone has to update prices in templates manually, which defeats the purpose.
- The countdown timer must reflect actual offer terms. Setting "offer valid 72 hours" and then extending it on request undermines guest trust.
- **GDPR note:** the tracking of proposal opens constitutes data processing. Ensure this is covered in your privacy policy.
