# Case Study: Marketing Automation & Guest Feedback Management

## Context

Hotel marketing teams frequently operate in silos: email blasts go to the entire database without segmentation, negative reviews sit unanswered for days, and the link between a marketing campaign and actual bookings is impossible to measure. The result is budget spent without accountability.

## Objective

Embed marketing and reputation management into the property's CRM so that guest communications are personalised, review responses are systematic, and marketing spend is attributable to revenue.

## What Was Done

### 1. Guest Base Segmentation

Using combined data from the PMS and CRM, the following segments were built:
- by visit frequency (new / returning / VIP),
- by booking channel (direct / OTA),
- by guest profile (families, couples, corporate),
- by geography (domestic / key international markets),
- by recency (at-risk guests: no return in 12+ months).

Each segment receives relevant, targeted communications — not mass blasts.

### 2. Automated Marketing Triggers

| Trigger | Segment | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Check-out | All guests | Email requesting a review |
| 60 days without a rebooking | Returning guests | Special offer for next stay |
| Pre-season | All active guests | Early booking promotion |
| Guest birthday | All profiles with DOB | Personalised greeting + discount |
| Post-negative review | Dissatisfied guests | Recovery offer from GM |

### 3. Review & Feedback Management

All reviews — from Booking.com, Expedia, Google, TripAdvisor, and direct channels — are aggregated into a single task queue. A responsible team member receives each review as a task with a response deadline.

For negative reviews, a priority routing is applied:
- automatic notification to the department head and GM,
- response templates for common complaint types,
- mandatory completion tracking (unresponded reviews flagged automatically).

Key metrics tracked: average response time, % of reviews answered within 24h, rating trend by platform.

### 4. Campaign Attribution

Dashboards provide:
- open rate and click-through rate by campaign,
- conversion from email to booking (via UTM tracking + CRM attribution),
- cost of re-acquisition per guest by segment,
- OTA vs direct channel rating comparison.

## Results

- Personalised campaigns convert at significantly higher rates than untargeted broadcasts.
- Review response time improved materially: no more reviews sitting unanswered for multiple days.
- Marketing spend is tied to bookings through CRM attribution — unprofitable channels become visible.

## Key Considerations

- Segmentation quality depends entirely on data quality. If the PMS doesn't pass complete guest data to the CRM, segments will be incomplete and misleading.
- **GDPR / CAN-SPAM / CASL compliance:** marketing emails require explicit opt-in from guests, a clear unsubscribe mechanism, and a documented retention policy for guest data. This is not optional.
- Reputation management is a distinct skill. Response templates need to be drafted in advance, and staff need training on handling conflict in a public forum.
- Responding to reviews on Google and Booking.com directly affects search ranking and conversion — treat it as a revenue function, not a PR one.
