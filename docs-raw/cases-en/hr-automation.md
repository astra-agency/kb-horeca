# Case Study: HR Process Automation for Hotels

## Context

In properties with 50+ staff, the HR department drowns in manual work: leave requests arrive via personal messaging apps, hire paperwork is emailed back and forth, and new employee onboarding depends on whether the HR manager remembered to print the right checklist that morning.

## Objective

Move core HR workflows into structured digital processes: automate task routing, approvals, and notifications so HR staff spend time on people — not administration.

## What Was Automated

### 1. New Employee Onboarding

A workflow is triggered the moment a hire is confirmed, automatically:
1. Creating tasks for each department: IT (provision accounts and devices), Facilities (prepare workstation), HR (complete employment documentation).
2. Sending the new hire a welcome email with instructions and a first-day schedule.
3. Assigning a buddy/mentor and scheduling check-in tasks at day 1, 7, and 30.
4. Triggering a probation review workflow at the end of the probationary period.

Nothing slips through — every step has an owner and a deadline.

### 2. Offboarding

The departure process is fully digitised:
- IT receives an automatic task to revoke system access on the final day,
- the line manager is tasked with receiving a handover of responsibilities,
- a digital clearance checklist replaces paper forms,
- Payroll is notified automatically for final settlement.

No more lost paper clearance sheets or forgotten access revocations.

### 3. Leave, Time Off, and Business Travel

An employee submits a request via a system form. From there:
- the line manager receives an approval task with a deadline,
- on approval — Payroll is notified and the absence is added to the shared team calendar,
- on rejection — the employee receives an automatic notification with the manager's comment.

Managers view a consolidated absence calendar for their team before approving — no more approving leave "by feel" without knowing who else is out.

## Results

- Onboarding administrative time reduced by 40–60% per new hire.
- Zero incidents of new staff arriving without system access or a prepared workstation.
- Leave requests no longer arrive in managers' personal WhatsApp or Slack DMs.
- A complete, searchable HR event history exists for every employee.

## Key Considerations

- Automation only works if the underlying processes are documented first. Define the workflow on paper, then build it in the system.
- Change management matters: staff need to learn to use the system rather than their old habits. Expect a 2–3 month transition period.
- Integration with payroll software (e.g. ADP, Gusto, Sage) is essential — otherwise HR will duplicate data entry manually.
- **Labour law compliance:** automated leave approval workflows must respect jurisdiction-specific rules (e.g. mandatory notice periods, statutory leave entitlements under EU Working Time Directive or US FMLA).
