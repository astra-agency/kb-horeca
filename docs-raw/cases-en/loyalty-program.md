# Case Study: Hotel Guest Loyalty Programme

## Context

OTAs have captured the loyalty dynamic: guests accumulate points on Booking.com or Expedia — not with a specific hotel. The property pays a commission of 15–25% on every booking, including guests who have stayed before and were satisfied. Without a direct loyalty relationship, repeat guests are effectively re-acquired at full OTA cost every time.

## Objective

Build a proprietary loyalty programme that incentivises guests to book directly and return to this property — rather than the next one with a better points deal on an aggregator.

## What Was Done

### 1. Points Accumulation System

Guests earn points on every stay proportional to their total spend. Points are stored in the guest's profile and can be applied to future bookings.

Programme rules:
- **Earn rate:** X points per $10/€10 spent,
- **Redemption:** points can offset up to Y% of a booking value,
- **Expiry:** points remain active as long as the guest stays at least once every 24 months.

### 2. Status Tier Structure

Guests are assigned a tier based on cumulative spend or number of stays:

| Tier | Entry Threshold | Perks |
|---|---|---|
| Member | Any stay | Points accumulation |
| Silver | $2,000 / 3 stays | 4% room rate discount, priority check-in |
| Gold | $7,500 / 10 stays | 7% discount, room upgrade on availability, complimentary transfer |
| Platinum | $20,000+ / 25 stays | 10% discount, guaranteed upgrade, dedicated host |

The guest sees their current tier and progress to the next level in their profile.

### 3. Members-Only Offers

Programme participants receive:
- advance access to seasonal promotions before they go public,
- exclusive rates not available on OTAs (enforcing direct booking value),
- personalised birthday offers.

### 4. Guest Profile Portal

Participants access their loyalty status via a personal portal:
- current tier and points balance,
- progress bar to the next tier,
- transaction history (earned and redeemed points),
- upcoming reservations.

## Results

- Direct booking share among returning guests increases: participants see a clear financial incentive to bypass OTAs.
- Average spend per stay rises among higher-tier members who more frequently add ancillary services.
- Guest database quality improves: programme members voluntarily complete their profiles.

## Key Considerations

- A loyalty programme is a long-term investment. Meaningful results typically take 6–12 months to materialise.
- **Programme economics must be stress-tested** before launch: points value, redemption cap, and maximum discount must be modelled to avoid creating a loss-making scheme.
- **Communication is everything:** if guests don't know the programme exists, it doesn't work. Build awareness into the booking confirmation, pre-arrival email, and check-in conversation.
- **GDPR / CCPA:** running a loyalty programme involves collecting and storing guest preference and transaction data. Ensure lawful basis, privacy notices, and data subject request processes are in place.
- The programme should be meaningfully better than OTA loyalty alternatives for guests who stay regularly — otherwise there's no reason to switch behaviour.
