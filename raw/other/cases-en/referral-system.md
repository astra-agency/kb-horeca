# Case Study: Guest Referral Programme

## Context

Word-of-mouth has always been the most trusted acquisition channel in hospitality. A referral programme is its digitised, trackable version: a guest shares a personal link, brings in a new booking, and earns a reward. The hotel acquires a pre-qualified guest at a cost far below OTA commission — while the referring guest strengthens their connection to the property.

## Objective

Build a referral mechanic embedded in the loyalty programme: unique personal links, automatic reward processing, and transparent tracking for the referring guest.

## What Was Done

### 1. Personal Referral Links

Every loyalty programme member receives a unique referral link in their guest portal. The link can be copied or shared directly via Telegram, WhatsApp, email, or social media.

When a new guest books using the link, the source is automatically attributed in the system.

### 2. Two-Sided Incentive Structure

The programme rewards both parties:

| Party | Reward |
|---|---|
| Referrer (the existing guest) | % of the new guest's first booking value — credited to their points balance |
| New guest | % bonus points on their first stay booked via the referral link |

Both rewards are processed automatically after the referred guest's stay is completed — not at booking. This protects against cancellation abuse.

### 3. Referral Dashboard in the Guest Portal

The referring guest sees in their portal:
- number of friends who registered via their link,
- points earned and credited,
- points pending (awaiting the referred guest's check-out).

Transparency drives sharing behaviour: guests who can see their referral performance share their link more actively.

### 4. Influencer & Content Creator Partnerships

The referral mechanic is particularly effective for influencer collaborations: a content creator receives a personal referral link and shares it with their audience. The hotel sees the exact number of bookings generated per creator and can calculate the actual ROI of the partnership — no reliance on reach estimates or brand awareness metrics.

### 5. Programme Analytics

Key metrics for managing the programme:
- number of active referrers,
- conversion rate: referral link click → completed booking,
- average booking value of referred guests vs other channels,
- cost of acquisition via referral vs OTA commission.

## Results

- The referral channel delivers a consistent stream of new guests at below-average acquisition cost.
- Referring guests exhibit higher loyalty metrics: they are invested in the hotel's success.
- Influencer partnerships become commercially accountable, with a direct revenue figure attached to each collaboration.

## Key Considerations

- The programme only works at scale if there is already a base of satisfied, engaged guests. Launching on an empty database yields nothing.
- **Reward value calibration:** the referral bonus must be attractive enough to motivate sharing, but not so generous that it creates exploitation risk (fake accounts, self-referrals).
- **Fraud prevention:** tie reward processing to completed stays rather than bookings; limit one programme account per email/device; flag unusual referral volume patterns.
- **GDPR:** the referral email or link-sharing mechanism must not send marketing to third parties without their consent. Design the flow so the new guest explicitly opts in.
