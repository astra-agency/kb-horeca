# Case Study: Legal & Contract Workflow Automation

## Context

In mid-size and large hotels, the legal and procurement teams face the same recurring problems: a contract is "stuck" somewhere waiting for a signature, nobody knows which approval stage it's at, and the contracts registry lives in a spreadsheet that is perpetually out of date.

## Objective

Build a transparent, end-to-end digital process for contract review, approval, signing, and storage — with automatic task routing and a single searchable document registry.

## What Was Done

### 1. Process Mapping

A complete inventory of document types and approval routes was created:
- supplier and vendor contracts,
- agency and distribution agreements (OTAs, tour operators, corporate accounts),
- lease and facilities agreements,
- NDAs and one-off documents.

For each type: defined approvers, approval sequence, and deadline per stage.

### 2. Approval & Signing Workflow

The originator (sales manager, procurement, etc.) submits the document via a system form. It then moves automatically through the approval chain:
- each approver receives a task with a deadline,
- if the deadline passes — automatic escalation to the approver's manager,
- if rejected — automatic return to the originator with comments,
- once fully approved — a final signing task is issued.

Every stakeholder sees the real-time status of any document without needing to ask.

### 3. Contract Registry

After signing, the document is automatically added to the registry with:
- date, reference number, counterparty, contract type,
- responsible manager,
- expiry date — with automatic reminders 30 and 7 days before expiry.

Search takes seconds. No more calls to Legal asking "do we actually have a contract with this vendor?"

## Results

- Average contract turnaround time reduced by 30–50%.
- Management visibility into bottlenecks: which approvers most frequently cause delays.
- Zero expired contracts renewed late — the reminder system catches them in advance.
- Legal team spends significantly less time fielding "what's the status?" requests.

## Key Considerations

- The biggest implementation challenge is discipline: if senior stakeholders don't action tasks in the system, the digital process is no better than paper.
- **E-signatures** (DocuSign, Adobe Sign, HelloSign) dramatically accelerate the process for both internal documents and counterparty agreements — and are legally valid in the EU (eIDAS) and US (ESIGN Act).
- **GDPR/data residency:** contracts often contain personal data. Ensure the document storage solution complies with data residency requirements relevant to your jurisdiction.
- Integration with cloud storage (SharePoint, Google Drive) simplifies retrieval of signed originals.
