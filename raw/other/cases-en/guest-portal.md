# Case Study: Guest Self-Service Portal

## Context

Most hotel guest touchpoints are mediated by third parties: OTAs store booking history, email is the only communication channel, and checking a loyalty points balance means calling the front desk. The hotel has no direct digital relationship with the guest outside of the stay itself.

## Objective

Build a personalised web portal where guests manage their entire hotel relationship: reservations, billing, loyalty, ancillary services, and online check-in — in one place, without calling or emailing.

## What Was Done

### 1. Reservation Management

The guest sees all reservations — upcoming and historical. For each booking:
- stay details and confirmation documents,
- payment status and option to pay the outstanding balance online,
- downloadable invoice or receipt,
- online check-in button (available 24–48 hours before arrival).

### 2. Online Check-In (Pre-Arrival Registration)

24–48 hours before check-in, the guest completes a digital pre-registration:
- confirms personal details,
- uploads a photo of their ID document (where legally required),
- selects preferences (floor, bed configuration, view).

Front desk receives the completed pre-registration and prepares the room accordingly. Physical check-in time at the desk drops to 2–3 minutes for pre-registered guests.

> **Note (EU/UK):** guest ID registration requirements vary by country. In many EU jurisdictions, hotels are legally required to collect ID data at check-in. Ensure your pre-registration flow complies with local regulations and that ID data is stored securely and deleted per your GDPR retention schedule.

### 3. Ancillary Service Ordering

Before or during the stay, the guest orders from the portal:
- early check-in / late check-out,
- airport or city transfer,
- meal plan upgrades,
- spa appointments or activity bookings,
- room decoration for special occasions.

Orders route automatically to the relevant department — no phone calls required.

### 4. Loyalty Programme Access

Within the portal, the guest sees:
- current tier and points balance,
- progress bar to the next tier,
- transaction history (earned / redeemed),
- personalised offers and exclusive member rates.

### 5. Resort Credit & Internal Currency

For resort properties or all-inclusive hotels operating an internal spend system, the portal displays:
- current credit balance,
- itemised transaction history,
- option to transfer credit to another guest in the same booking (e.g. a family member at a different pool bar).

### 6. In-Stay Communication

The guest can:
- submit a maintenance request or service preference,
- message the concierge,
- view the activities schedule and event calendar.

## Results

- Front desk inquiry volume for routine information (points balance, payment status, service requests) decreases measurably.
- Ancillary revenue conversion improves: presenting an upgrade or transfer offer three days before arrival outperforms a verbal offer at check-in.
- Direct guest data collection improves: portal users voluntarily complete profiles more thoroughly than OTA-sourced guests.

## Key Considerations

- Without PMS integration, the portal is not viable. Reservation data must sync in real time — a static snapshot is insufficient.
- **Onboarding:** guests need to know the portal exists and understand its value. Introduce it at booking confirmation and in the pre-arrival email.
- **Mobile-first design is non-negotiable:** 70–80% of guests will access hotel communications via smartphone. The portal must perform flawlessly on mobile.
- **GDPR / CCPA:** the portal involves collecting, storing, and processing personal data. Ensure your privacy notice, consent mechanisms, and data subject request process are fully implemented.
