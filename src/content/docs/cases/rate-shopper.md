## Context

Revenue managers spent 1–2 hours daily manually checking competitor rates on OTAs. By the time rates were adjusted, the demand window had often passed.

## Objective

Automate competitor rate monitoring and integrate alerts into the daily revenue management workflow so rates can be adjusted within minutes, not hours.

## Results

- Manual rate monitoring: eliminated (was 1–2 hours/day)
- Average rate adjustment lag: 4 hours → 15 minutes
- Direct revenue increase: +15% through faster market response
