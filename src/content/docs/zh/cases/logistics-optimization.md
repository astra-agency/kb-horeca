A large hotel and restaurant chain with 25 locations struggled with inefficient distribution networks, high transportation costs, and poor inventory management. Supply chain delays were impacting operations and customer satisfaction.

## Business Challenge

- 25 locations with different suppliers and delivery schedules
- No centralized distribution center or network optimization
- High transportation costs due to inefficient routing
- Unpredictable delivery times affecting operations
- Poor visibility into supply chain status
- Warehouse space inefficiencies at each location
- Damaged goods and product loss during shipping
- Difficulty forecasting demand and planning logistics

## Solution Implemented

The team developed a comprehensive logistics optimization platform:

- Regional distribution centers with optimized inventory levels
- AI-powered demand forecasting and inventory planning
- Route optimization for deliveries and reduced transportation costs
- Real-time tracking of shipments and inventory in transit
- Automated order fulfillment and warehouse management
- Quality control checks at distribution centers
- Supplier integration for better coordination
- Analytics dashboard for logistics performance monitoring

## Results Achieved

- Reduced transportation costs by 25%
- Improved delivery reliability to 96% on-time
- Decreased inventory holding costs by 18%
- Reduced inventory write-offs from spoilage by 40%
- Improved warehouse efficiency through centralized distribution
- Better demand forecasting accuracy (within 5%)
- Reduced order fulfillment time from 5 days to 2 days
- Enhanced visibility and traceability across the supply chain

## Key Learnings

- Centralized distribution centers improve efficiency
- Demand forecasting reduces excess inventory and stockouts
- Route optimization significantly reduces transportation costs
- Real-time tracking improves visibility and responsiveness
- Quality control at distribution centers prevents losses
- Supplier integration enables better planning and coordination
- Analytics help identify and address bottlenecks
