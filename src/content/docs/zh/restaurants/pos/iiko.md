## About

**iiko** is one of the leading restaurant automation systems in Russia. Used in restaurants, cafés, bars, fast-food chains, and dark kitchens. Full cycle: from order taking to food cost management and staff control.

## Key products

### iikoRMS (Restaurant Management System)

Core restaurant management system:
- Order taking and routing
- Cash register operations and fiscal compliance
- Menu and pricing management
- Staff: working hours, motivation, access levels

### iikoChain

Multi-location chain management from a single hub:
- Centralized menu and pricing
- Consolidated reporting
- Chain-wide inventory management

### iikoDelivery

Dedicated delivery module:
- Aggregate orders from all channels (website, aggregators, phone)
- Courier route optimization
- Delivery time tracking

### iikoFront

Cash register terminal supporting:
- Modifiers and combos
- Discounts and promo codes
- Card, SBP fast payment, and cash

### iikoKitchen (KDS)

Kitchen display system — manages dish preparation queue.

## Inventory and analytics

- Inventory management and food cost tracking
- Recipe cards for all dishes
- Menu ABC analysis
- Revenue, average check, and table turnover reports

## Integrations

- Yandex Food, Delivery Club (delivery aggregators)
- Loyalty systems (native iiko Card + third-party)
- Hotels: TravelLine, Logus HMS
- Accounting (1C)
- Fiscal registers (Atol, Evotor, and others)
- CRM: Bitrix24
