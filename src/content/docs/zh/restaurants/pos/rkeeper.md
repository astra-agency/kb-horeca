## About

**r_keeper** is a restaurant automation system for cafés, restaurants, bars, and any food service venue. More than **70,000 venues** in Russia and CIS. Operating since 1992.

## Products

### r_keeper Cloud

Cloud solution for small cafés and restaurants.
- **Price:** from ₽4,085/mo
- Fast start, no installation required
- Core features: cash register, orders, inventory, analytics

### r_keeper LifeTime

Modular system for chains and large venues.
- **Price:** from ₽107,000 (one-time)
- Flexible modularity
- Suitable for franchises and chains

### r_keeper Delivery

Delivery management module:
- Courier route optimization
- Aggregator integrations
- Time and status tracking

### r_keeper WebManager

Web-based management — reports and settings from anywhere.

## Key features

- Order management and cash register operations
- Food cost control and inventory management
- Sales analytics and reporting
- Offline mode (works without internet)
- Mobile terminals (tablets, smartphones)
- 24/7 technical support

## Best for

Coffee shops, fast food, bars, dark kitchens, chain restaurants, franchises.
