## Context

Guest acquisition relied heavily on OTAs and paid advertising. Word-of-mouth referrals happened organically but were never tracked or incentivized.

## Objective

Create a structured referral program that gives existing guests a reason to recommend the hotel and tracks conversions automatically.

## Results

- 120 new bookings per month from referrals within 6 months
- Customer acquisition cost via referrals: 3× lower than paid channels
- Referred guests showed 28% higher retention rate
