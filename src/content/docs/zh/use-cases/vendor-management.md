A hotel chain with 15 locations faced challenges in managing multiple vendors across different regions, leading to inconsistent service quality and inflated costs.

## Business Challenge

- 15 hotel locations with different vendors for same services
- No centralized vendor database or performance tracking
- Vendors negotiated independently, leading to price inconsistencies
- Poor communication and delayed deliveries
- No quality control or SLA tracking
- Duplicate vendors across regions
- Manual invoice processing and payment reconciliation

## Solution Implemented

The team developed a comprehensive vendor management system:

- Centralized vendor database with performance metrics
- Vendor evaluation framework and scoring system
- Automated RFQ (Request for Quotation) process
- Standardized contracts and terms across locations
- Performance dashboards tracking delivery and quality metrics
- Integrated payment and invoice processing
- Vendor portal for order tracking and communication
- Periodic vendor reviews and optimization

## Results Achieved

- Consolidated vendors leading to 20% cost savings
- Improved delivery performance from 85% to 98% on-time
- Reduced payment processing time by 70%
- Better vendor relationships through transparent SLAs
- Eliminated duplicate vendors and redundant services
- Centralized pricing saved $200,000 annually
- Improved communication and reduced order errors

## Key Learnings

- Vendor consolidation and standardization drive significant savings
- Transparent performance metrics improve service quality
- Automated processes reduce administrative overhead
- Data-driven vendor evaluation improves purchasing decisions
- Clear SLAs and contracts prevent conflicts and misunderstandings
