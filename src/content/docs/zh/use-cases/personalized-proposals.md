## Context

Corporate sales relied on generic email templates. Proposals took 2–3 hours to prepare and looked identical regardless of client segment.

## Objective

Automate the creation of personalized proposals that pull in client history, preferred room types, and relevant upsell packages.

## Results

- Proposal preparation time reduced from 2 hours to 15 minutes
- Conversion rate increased from 12% to 31%
- Average deal value increased by 22% through contextual upsells
