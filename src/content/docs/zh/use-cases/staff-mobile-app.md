## Context

Staff communication relied on radio, paper checklists, and WhatsApp groups. Task assignments were verbal, completions untracked, and priorities unclear during peak periods.

## Objective

Give every staff member a mobile tool for receiving tasks, logging completions, reporting issues, and communicating with supervisors — replacing paper and informal channels.

## Results

- Task completion tracking: 0% → 94% of all assignments
- Guest request response time reduced by 35%
- Paper checklists eliminated across 3 departments
