## About

**Saby Hotel** (formerly SBIS.Hotel) is a PMS within the Saby business platform. Full automation: reservations, billing, housekeeping, restaurant, accounting, HR — in one interface. Scales from a small hostel to a large chain.

## Core modules

### Reservation management
- Interactive room chart with guest name and amount on hover
- Room search assistant by dates and guest count
- Auto-fill guest data from passport photo
- Checkout in 2 clicks with automatic housekeeping task creation

### Payments and billing
- All payment types: cash, card, SBP (fast pay), online, invoice, mixed
- Automatic tourist tax calculation
- One-button receipt — PMS, fiscal register, and bank terminal simultaneously

### Online bookings
- Website and social media widget (5-minute setup)
- No OTA commission, custom branding
- OTA connections: Booking.com, Yandex Travel, OneTwoTrip, and more

### Reputation management (SabyGPT)
- Aggregate reviews from multiple platforms in one inbox
- Positive reviews published automatically
- Negative reviews visible only to owner, with AI-generated response suggestions via SabyGPT

### Staff management
- Time tracking: system login, access cards, biometrics
- Flexible shift scheduling with automatic payroll calculation
- KPIs, ratings, staff badges
- Video surveillance integration (linked to receipts)

### Analytics
- Real-time payment tracking
- Occupancy, revenue, expenses — with filters
- Mobile app for manager (iOS/Android)

- Form 1-KSR auto-filled from booking data
- Tourist tax calculated automatically
