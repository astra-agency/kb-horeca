## Summary

**TravelLine** is the market leader with a complete tool stack (PMS + Channel Manager + Booking Engine + Reputation + website builder). Best for 3–5★ hotels that need deep integration across all sales channels.

**Bnovo** is a modern cloud PMS with excellent UX and a built-in channel manager. Best choice for small and mid-size properties: hostels, glamping, aparthotels.

## Market position

TravelLine and HRS (ex-Libra) together hold ~75% of HotelTech revenue in Russia. Bnovo is the third major player with ~₽0.42B revenue. In 2024 Bnovo acquired Smartway, which triggered pricing changes.

## TravelLine advantages

- Widest tool stack on one platform
- Powerful Booking Engine with direct-sales mechanics (promo codes, upgrades, countdown timers)
- Reputation Manager with AI review analysis
- 300+ integrations (CRM, restaurants, locks, document scanners)
- Strong track record with large properties and chains

## Bnovo advantages

- Modern interface, excellent mobile app
- Channel Manager and Booking Engine included in subscription
- Dynamic pricing out of the box
- Larger property count (16,000+) — wider OTA integration network
- More affordable for small properties

## Choose TravelLine when

- City hotel 3–5★ with strong focus on direct sales
- Need reputation monitoring in one system
- Using iiko or r_keeper for restaurant operations
- Want a single vendor for the entire stack

## Choose Bnovo when

- Hostel, glamping, aparthotel, resort
- Need convenient mobile access for staff
- Small team — value simplicity and speed
- Want Channel Manager without extra monthly cost
