## Why reputation management matters

Ratings on Yandex Maps, 2GIS, and TripAdvisor directly affect booking conversion. Improving a rating from 4.2 to 4.6 typically yields **+15–30% in direct bookings**.

## What reputation systems automate

- Automatic review request after checkout (SMS, email, messaging apps)
- Aggregate reviews from all platforms in one interface
- New review notifications
- AI-generated response drafts
- Analytics — trends, problem areas, rating dynamics over time
