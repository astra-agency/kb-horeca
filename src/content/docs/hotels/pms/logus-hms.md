## About

**Logus HMS** is a PMS from Libra Hospitality (Russian development, 2014). Client-server architecture: installed on the hotel's own server. Operates fully without internet. Built for large properties (100+ rooms) and hotel chains.

## Architecture

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | On-premise (client-server) |
| Internet required | No — works autonomously |
| Speed | High (local server) |
| Updates | By contract with Libra Hospitality |

## Core modules

### Room inventory management
- Rooms and bed-spaces — both as sellable units
- Pricing by nights, days, or hours
- Room blocks, overbooking limits
- Split billing for guests sharing a room at different rates

### Rate management
- Flexible base rates with auto-correction of linked prices (corporate/agent)
- Seasonal rates, length-of-stay rates
- Service package management

### Corporate clients and agencies
- Self-service portal for corporate partners and travel agencies
- Manage room blocks, quotas, rates independently
- Create/cancel reservations with automatic PMS reflection

### Group bookings
- Group management, events, conference room management
- Dedicated banquets and events module

### Restaurant integrations
- Native integration with iiko and r_keeper
- Room charge (transfer restaurant bill to room account)

### Multi-property reporting
- Consolidated reports for chains
- Detailed analytics per property
