## About

**Bnovo** is a cloud PMS for hotels, aparthotels, hostels, resorts, glamping sites, and sanatoriums. Used by **16,000+ properties**.

Claimed results after implementation: +25% revenue, +30% ADR, +30% staff efficiency.

## Modules

### Bnovo PMS
- Interactive room chart — occupancy and housekeeping status
- Dynamic pricing and competitor rate analysis
- Revenue management tools
- Mobile app for staff

### Booking module
- Direct sales via website, social media, maps, messaging apps
- Room upgrade and add-ons at booking stage
- Smart availability calendar with suggestions

### Channel Manager
- Single interface for all OTAs
- Real-time synchronization, overbooking protection
- Automatic inventory updates

## Add-on modules

- **Bnovo Pay** — payment gateway
- Electronic locks
- Housekeeping management
- Police/FMS guest registration
- IP telephony
- Reporting and analytics

## Integrations

- OTA: Ostrovok, Yandex Travel, Avito, Booking.com, Airbnb
- CRM: Bitrix24, AmoCRM
- Fiscal registers (online cash)
