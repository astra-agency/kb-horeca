## About

**MaxiBooking** (maxi-booking.ru) is a cloud hotel management system. No limit on the number of rooms or properties per account. Free 15-day trial.

## Key features

- PMS — full control of accommodation operations
- Channel Manager — synchronization across all sales channels
- Automated reservation and sales management
- Optimal room matching for guests
- Works from any device and browser

## Best for

- Hotels and guesthouses of any size
- Small hotels and B&Bs
- Hostels
- Resorts and sanatoriums
- Short-term apartment and aparthotel rentals
