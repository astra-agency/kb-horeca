## Step 1 — Match the system to your venue type

| Venue type | Recommended |
|---|---|
| Coffee shop / food truck | Cloud POS on iPad: Poster Startup or r_keeper Cloud |
| Café up to 50 seats | Poster Business or iiko Cloud |
| Restaurant 50+ seats | iiko Cloud or r_keeper |
| Bar / nightclub | r_keeper (strong bar-mode cash module) |
| Chain of 3+ locations | iiko Chain or r_keeper LifeTime |
| Dark kitchen / delivery-only | iiko Delivery or r_keeper Delivery |

## Step 2 — Required features checklist

- [ ] Offline mode — POS keeps taking orders when internet drops
- [ ] Kitchen display system (KDS) or kitchen printer
- [ ] Inventory management and recipe cards
- [ ] Staff management — schedules, access levels, performance tracking
- [ ] Fiscal compliance (online cash register + fiscal data operator)
- [ ] Alcohol license tracking (if you sell alcohol)
- [ ] Delivery platform integrations (Yandex Food, Delivery Club, own delivery)
- [ ] Loyalty program — built-in or via integration
- [ ] Owner mobile app for remote monitoring

## Step 3 — Questions to ask every vendor

1. **How does the POS behave when internet goes down?** — Must continue accepting orders
2. **What's in the base plan vs paid add-ons?** — KDS, mobile terminals, API integrations
3. **How is support delivered?** — Chat, phone, response time, SLA guarantees
4. **How are system updates applied?** — Automatic or requires IT involvement?
5. **Do you migrate data from our current system?** — Menu items, recipes, guest database
6. **Which hardware is compatible?** — Terminals, printers, cash drawers, KDS screens
7. **How does it integrate with accounting software?** — 1C, or export formats

## Step 4 — Total cost of ownership (TCO)

Don't evaluate on subscription price alone:

| Cost item | iiko | r_keeper | Poster |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly subscription | from ₽5,990/mo | from ₽4,085/mo | from ₽1,120/mo |
| Hardware | ₽15,000–60,000 | ₽15,000–60,000 | ₽5,000–30,000 |
| Setup & implementation | ₽10,000–50,000 | ₽15,000–100,000 | ₽0–10,000 |
| Staff training | Included / partners | Via partners | Self-service |
| Fiscal operator fee | ~₽3,000/yr | ~₽3,000/yr | ~₽3,000/yr |

## Step 5 — Test before you buy

All three major systems offer trial access:
- **iiko** — 30-day free trial
- **r_keeper** — demo on request
- **Poster** — 15-day free trial

**What to test during the trial:**
- How fast a waiter creates and sends an order
- How a receipt is printed and the order appears in the kitchen
- How readable and useful the daily reports are
- How dish modifications work (no onions, add sauce)

## Red flags

- Vendor won't give a live demo — walk away
- No offline mode for the cash register — critical operational risk
- Support hours are business hours only — restaurants operate at night
- Extra charge for every integration — calculate real total cost
- No menu migration when switching systems — vendor lock-in
