## Overview

Opening and operating a HoReCa business in Russia requires several permits and registrations. Requirements vary by region — always verify current rules with local regulatory authorities.

:::note
This page contains general information. For advice on specific situations, consult a lawyer or compliance specialist.
:::

## Business registration

Before starting operations:

- Register an LLC (ООО) or sole proprietor (ИП) with the tax authority
- Obtain a Tax ID (ИНН) and registration number (ОГРН / ОГРНИП)
- Choose a tax regime (general, simplified, patent)
- Open a business bank account
- Register with the Pension Fund and Social Insurance Fund (if employing staff)

## Rospotrebnadzor notification

Before opening a food service establishment, you **must** file a notification with Rospotrebnadzor (Federal Service for Consumer Rights) about the start of operations (Government Resolution No. 584).

File via:
- In person at the local Rospotrebnadzor office
- MFC (multifunctional service center)
- Gosuslugi portal

## Alcohol license

If the venue sells alcoholic beverages, a license from Rosalkogolregulirovanie (Federal Alcohol Regulatory Service) is required.

| License type | Description |
|---|---|
| Retail sale (food service) | Selling alcohol in a bar/restaurant for on-premises consumption |
| Retail sale (off-license) | Selling alcohol to take away |
| Production | For alcohol producers |

Processing time: up to 30 business days. State fee: ₽65,000 per year. More: [rosalcohol.ru](https://rosalcohol.ru).

## Staff health requirements

All employees who handle food must:

- Hold a valid **personal health record (medical book)**
- Pass medical examinations per Ministry of Health Order No. 302n
- Complete **hygiene training and certification** (sanitary minimum)

## Cash register compliance (54-FZ)

All customer payments must be processed through an **online cash register** under Federal Law 54-FZ. The register must be:

- Registered with the Federal Tax Service (FNS)
- Connected to a Fiscal Data Operator (OFD)
- Equipped with a fiscal storage device

More: [Regulations → 54-FZ](/regulations/54-fz/)

## EGAIS (alcohol tracking)

Venues that handle alcohol must connect to **EGAIS** (Unified State Automated Alcohol Accounting System).

More: [Regulations → EGAIS](/regulations/egais/)

## Labor law

All employers must comply with the **Russian Labor Code (TK RF)**:

- Minimum wage requirements
- Working hours and overtime limits
- Leave entitlements (annual, sick, maternity/paternity)
- Anti-discrimination and workplace safety obligations
- HR documentation and payslip requirements

For labor disputes: **Rostrud** — [rostrud.gov.ru](https://rostrud.gov.ru)

## Inspections

Rospotrebnadzor conducts scheduled and unscheduled inspections. To pass successfully:

- Maintain all required logs (temperature control, cleaning, HACCP)
- Keep staff medical records current
- Keep the premises clean and in good repair
- Resolve technical issues promptly
