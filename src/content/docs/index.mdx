## Welcome to the HoReCa Knowledge Base

This knowledge base provides practical guidance for professionals working in the **Hotel, Restaurant, and Café (HoReCa)** industry. Whether you are managing a boutique hotel, a busy restaurant, or a local café, you will find the information you need here.

## What's Covered

- **Operations** — Day-to-day front-of-house and back-of-house procedures
- **Inventory Management** — Stock control, ordering, and waste reduction
- **Staff Management** — Hiring, training, scheduling, and team communication
- **Food Safety** — HACCP principles, allergen management, and temperature control
- **Compliance** — Licensing, health inspections, and regulatory requirements
