## Context

Contracts were drafted from scratch each time. Approval chains ran through email. Deadline tracking was a calendar filled with manual reminders.

## Objective

Automate contract generation from templates, route approvals through a structured workflow, and track all deadlines in one system.

## Results

- Contract preparation time: 3 days → 4 hours
- Legal approval cycle reduced by 60%
- Zero missed contract renewal deadlines in the first year
