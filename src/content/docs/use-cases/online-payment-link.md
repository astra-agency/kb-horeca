## Context

Collecting prepayments required manual bank transfer instructions, confirmation tracking, and frequent follow-ups. No-show rate on unconfirmed bookings was high.

## Objective

Enable guests to pay instantly from any device via a link sent in messenger or email — no bank details, no manual reconciliation.

## Results

- Prepayment collection time: 3 days → 2 hours
- No-show rate reduced by 40%
- Finance team time on payment reconciliation reduced by 70%
