## Context

A hotel without a centralized CRM works blind: sales managers lose leads, marketing doesn't know who has already been a guest, and analytics are compiled manually in Excel.

## Objective

Build a single control point for sales and guest communications: connect all incoming channels, set up automated touchpoint sequences, and configure funnel analytics.

## Results

- All incoming channels unified in one system
- Automated pre-arrival and post-stay sequences
- Sales funnel visibility from first contact to repeat booking
- Reduced response time from 4 hours to 20 minutes
